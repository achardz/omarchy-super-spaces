# Omarchy menu, shortcuts and clipboard

Start your search with a short word to look in just one place.

## `menu` — the Omarchy menu

Type `menu` to see Omarchy's whole menu (Apps, Learn, Appearance, Setup,
Install, System…), in the same order as the real one.

- `menu install` shows everything under Install.
- `menu docker`, `menu wifi`, `menu theme` jump to anything, however deep.
- **Enter** opens that page in the Omarchy menu, or runs the action.

You don't always need the word `menu`: typing `lock` already shows
**System › Lock** among your results.

> Items that depend on your system (like Hibernate) open their page instead
> of running directly, so Omarchy can decide what's available.

## `keys` — keyboard shortcuts

Type `keys` to see every Omarchy shortcut.

- `keys theme` → **Theme menu** with its keys.
- `keys super f` finds shortcuts by their keys.
- **Enter** runs the shortcut for you, exactly as if you'd pressed it.

## `clip` — clipboard history

Type `clip` to see what you copied recently.

- `clip tailscale` finds a past copy containing "tailscale".
- **Enter** pastes it into the window you were in.
- **Shift + Enter** only copies it.

> Your clipboard history can hold passwords or keys you copied. Delete those
> in Omarchy's clipboard manager (**Super + Ctrl + V**).

| Long | Short |
|---|---|
| `menu` | `om` |
